The payout math

The same fee structure, applied to your bookings

Avante charges a flat 15% platform fee on your property's booking volume — the standard rate across the network, whether you're an affiliate or listing your own property. The first R1,967 of monthly bookings covers platform hosting. From R1,967 to R10,000 the fee is capped there — no extra charge. Between R10,000 and R50,000, a standard 2.5% transaction fee applies on top. Above R50,000 in a single month, fees cap completely. Payouts are made monthly.

Questions

Before you apply

Do I need professional photos?

Avante doesn't supply professional photography for owner-listed properties — the photos you provide are what guests will see, so clear, well-lit images make a real difference to bookings.

Who can apply to list a property?

Anyone — there's no minimum portfolio size or prior listing history required. This is for the property owner or manager themselves, applying and running the listing directly.

How does availability get updated?

Once your rates are loaded, the Avante calendar handles availability automatically as bookings come in — there's no manual calendar management required.

How does Avante know a booking is for my property?

Every link generated in your Hub carries your property's unique ID. Any guest who books through it — or through your listing directly on Avante — is tracked back to your property.

When and how do I get paid?

Your net booking revenue, after the 15% platform fee, is paid out monthly to the bank details you keep updated in your Hub.

What does it cost to list?

There's no sign-up fee or software subscription. The only cost is the 15% platform fee, charged the same way across the network — see the payout breakdown above.
