Where your property actually shows up

Everything starts with Avante. From there, distribution and promotion happen on your behalf — across channel managers, niche platforms, and closed listings.

Our booking platform — the engine behind it all

Avante's own booking platform, built specifically for the African hospitality market, powers everything you see here. Every property — public or closed — is listed here first, and it's what powers the real-time links, affiliate tracking, and channel manager distribution described below.

Channel managers: NightsBridge and HTI

Already using NightsBridge or HTI (Hospitality Technology International)? We link straight into your existing setup, so your availability and rates stay in sync without any manual updating on your end. Not on a channel manager yet? We'll push your property out to the right platforms — but only if you want us to.

Closed properties

A niche option for owners who want the marketing reach without the open-market listing. Closed properties still sit on Avante's platform and still reach the affiliate network — an owner renting out a family home over the December holidays, for example, gets exposure to the right renters without a public search listing.

Niche showcase platforms

Beyond the core distribution, listed properties can also feature across a set of purpose-built showcase platforms.

Take A Break

A niche market for end-users looking for personalised holidays matched to their interests — golfing, fishing, wildlife, pet-friendly, and more.

Loyalty programs

Closed-group members receive exclusive, discounted rates, plus credit toward a Virtual Card on every transaction.

Independent agencies

Tailored platforms for independent travel agencies to improve service and facilitate bookings on your property.

Membership & travel clubs

Individuals join for an annual or monthly fee, unlocking savings on hospitality products via Avante Wealth.

NPO partnerships

NPOs — Non-Profit Organisations working toward a social or community cause rather than private profit — join as the sole group on a closed platform, giving their supporters and donors exclusive access.

Avante Travel

Our sister booking platform — the affiliate storefront that markets listed properties directly to travellers and drives bookings back to you.
